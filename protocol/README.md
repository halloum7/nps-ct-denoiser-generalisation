# Pre-registered protocol

The study protocol (hypotheses V0 and H1-H5, training conditions, test grid, metrics, statistical tests and success criteria) was written and dated on 14 November 2026, before any model training. Operational choices left open by the protocol were fixed on 21 November 2026, before any test-set inference; they are documented in the header of `code/stage2d_stats.py`. Amendments A1-A3, all made before the runs they concern, are recorded in the protocol with their dates and reasons.

File: `protocol_stage2_preregistered_fr.md` (protocol in French, exported from the authors' working document, revision 25). The working document was updated as the stages were completed: the Stage 1, 2a and 2b result sections and the dated amendments were added at those times, so this export is the current revision of the document, not a frozen copy of 14 November 2026.

The Stage 1, 2a and 2b results recorded in the protocol (emulation checks, family checks, pilot) are reproduced by `code/stage1_main.py`, `code/stage2a_main.py` and `code/stage2b_main.py`.
